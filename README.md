## Research Approach

Each case study considers:

- Transaction and position details
- Investment thesis
- Evidence available at or before entry
- Financial and operating indicators
- Key risks
- What happened after the investment
- Lessons learned

Later information is used to evaluate the original thesis and investment outcome rather than being presented as information that was known at the time of entry.
